GAMEWITHFUN SEO SETUP
=====================

Current homepage SEO:
- Title: GameWithFun - Free Online Games | Play Games Online
- Meta description: optimized for free online games / no download
- Robots: index, follow
- Open Graph title/description
- Twitter card metadata
- WebSite JSON-LD structured data
- Semantic H1 and section headings
- Search-friendly game/category wording

IMPORTANT:
Replace YOUR-DOMAIN.com in index.html with your real domain after you buy it.
Do not use a domain you do not own.

Next SEO steps:
1. Add your real domain to canonical and og:url.
2. Create robots.txt.
3. Create sitemap.xml.
4. Verify the domain in Google Search Console.
5. Submit sitemap.xml in Search Console.
6. Give every real game its own crawlable page/URL.
7. Use unique title + description for every game page.
8. Use original/licensed thumbnails and game content.
9. Keep mobile loading fast.
10. Add internal links between Home, Categories and game pages.
