Introduction

Welcome to GameWithFun. We respect your privacy and are committed to explaining how information may be handled when you visit and use our website, games, Google Login and leaderboard features.

🔐

Google Login

GameWithFun provides an optional "Login with Google" feature. You can use Google Login to access features such as saving game activity and participating in the GameWithFun leaderboard.

When you choose to sign in with Google, GameWithFun may receive basic information associated with your Google account, such as your name, email address and profile picture, depending on the information made available through your Google account.

GameWithFun does not request or store your Google account password.

Information We May Collect

When you use Google Login and our gaming features, GameWithFun may process limited information needed to provide these services.

  • Name or player name
  • Email address associated with Google Login
  • Profile picture, if available
  • Firebase user ID (UID)
  • Game scores and leaderboard activity
  • Basic technical and usage information

We do not request your Google password.

🏆

Leaderboard

GameWithFun provides an online gaming leaderboard where signed-in players may submit game scores.

When you participate in the leaderboard, your selected player name and game score may be displayed publicly.

The public leaderboard is designed to display player rankings and scores. Your email address, Google account details and Firebase UID are not displayed publicly on the leaderboard.

Firebase Services

GameWithFun uses Firebase services to provide authentication and database functionality.

Firebase Authentication may be used to provide Google Login, while Firebase Firestore may be used to store account-related information, game scores and leaderboard data.

Information processed through Firebase is handled according to Google's and Firebase's applicable policies and terms.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide Google Login.
  • Create and manage GameWithFun accounts.
  • Save game scores and leaderboard activity.
  • Display public leaderboard rankings.
  • Maintain and improve our games and website.
  • Protect the website from abuse and unauthorized activity.
  • Maintain website security and functionality.

Cookies and Local Storage

GameWithFun may use cookies, local storage and similar technologies to support website functionality, remember preferences, maintain login sessions and improve the user experience.

Third-party services used on the website may also use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

Advertising

GameWithFun may display advertisements from third-party advertising providers, including Google AdSense.

Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure and improve advertisements.

Advertising and cookie practices may change when additional services are added to GameWithFun.

Third-Party Services

GameWithFun may use third-party services such as:

  • Google Sign-In
  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Firestore
  • Google AdSense, if enabled
  • Analytics or security services, if enabled

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by GameWithFun. However, no internet service can guarantee complete security of information.

Data Retention and Deletion

Account information and leaderboard information may be retained as necessary to provide GameWithFun services.

If you would like to request deletion of your GameWithFun account or associated personal information, please contact us through our Contact Us page.

Children's Privacy

GameWithFun does not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe that personal information has been submitted improperly, please contact us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services or legal requirements change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
