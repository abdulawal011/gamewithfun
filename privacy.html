Introduction

Welcome to GameWithFun. We respect your privacy and are committed to explaining how information may be handled when you visit and use our website, games, Google Login, Favorites and leaderboard features.

GameWithFun is a browser-based gaming website. You can browse and play available games without creating an account. Google Login is optional and is used for supported leaderboard features.

🔐

Google Login

GameWithFun provides an optional "Login with Google" feature. You can use Google Login to participate in supported GameWithFun leaderboard features.

When you choose to sign in with Google, GameWithFun may receive basic information associated with your Google account, such as your display name, email address and profile picture, depending on the information made available through the authentication service.

GameWithFun does not request or store your Google account password.

Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use GameWithFun, the website may process limited information needed to provide authentication, leaderboard and other website features.

  • Name or display name
  • Email address associated with Google Login
  • Profile picture, if available
  • Firebase user ID (UID)
  • Games played
  • Tracked game activity time
  • Recently started or played game
  • Leaderboard-related information
  • Basic technical and usage information

We do not request or store your Google password.

🏆

Leaderboard

GameWithFun provides an optional online gaming leaderboard for participating players.

Players can use Google Login to participate in supported leaderboard features and have eligible game activity associated with their account.

The current GameWithFun leaderboard primarily uses tracked activity time rather than individual game scores.

Public leaderboard information may include a player's display name, profile picture, games played, tracked activity time, rank and activity status.

Your email address, Google account password and Firebase UID are not intended to be displayed publicly on the leaderboard.

⏱️

Game Activity and Time Tracking

When an authenticated player starts a supported game through GameWithFun, the website may record information about that game activity for leaderboard functionality.

This may include the game started, games played, recent activity and tracked activity time.

The current activity system is designed to count visible GameWithFun activity. When the browser page becomes hidden, such as when a visitor switches to another browser tab, hidden-tab time is not intended to be counted as active GameWithFun time.

Tracked activity time is primarily used for the GameWithFun leaderboard.

Leaderboard Periods

The GameWithFun leaderboard uses recurring periods during each month.

  • The first period covers days 1 through 15.
  • The second period begins on day 16 and continues through the final day of the month.

The current leaderboard period calculation uses the Asia/Riyadh time zone.

When a new leaderboard period begins, the relevant activity values may be reset for the new period.

Firebase Services

GameWithFun uses Firebase services to provide authentication and leaderboard-related functionality.

Firebase Authentication may be used to provide Google Login.

Firebase Firestore or related Firebase services may be used to store account-related information, game activity and leaderboard data.

Information processed through Firebase is handled according to Google's and Firebase's applicable policies and terms.

❤️

Favorites and Local Storage

GameWithFun provides a Favorites feature that allows visitors to save selected games for easier access later.

The current Favorites system uses the browser's local storage to save selected game information.

Favorites are stored locally in the browser and are separate from the Firebase leaderboard account.

If you clear your browser's site data or local storage, your saved Favorites may be removed.
