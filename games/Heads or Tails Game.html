Play Heads or Tails Online

Welcome to Heads or Tails, a free online coin flip game designed for quick and simple browser gameplay. Choose Heads or Tails, flip the virtual coin and check whether your prediction matches the result.

The game is designed to work smoothly on mobile phones, tablets and desktop browsers. No download or installation is required. Simply open the game, select a side and start flipping.

Heads or Tails is based on the familiar idea of a coin toss. Each round produces a random result, giving you a simple way to test your luck and try to create a longer winning streak.

How It Works

How to Play Heads or Tails

Playing the game is easy. First, choose either Heads or Tails. The virtual coin will then flip and reveal the result.

Choose Heads or Tails

Select the side you think will appear after the coin flip. You can make a new prediction for every round.

Flip the Coin

After making your choice, the coin performs an animated flip before showing the final result.

Build Your Streak

If your prediction matches the result, your win count and current streak increase. A wrong prediction ends the current winning streak.
