⚔️ Mini Knight

Mini Knight is a colorful action adventure game from GameWithFun. Control a brave little knight as he travels through a dangerous kingdom filled with monsters, traps, treasure and powerful bosses.

Fight your way through different areas, collect coins, protect yourself with your shield and use your sword to defeat enemies. Every stage becomes more challenging as you get closer to the final castle.

🎮 How to Play

  • Move your knight left and right.
  • Jump over gaps and dangerous obstacles.
  • Attack monsters with your sword.
  • Use your shield when an enemy attacks.
  • Collect coins and treasure.
  • Collect hearts to restore health.
  • Defeat enemies and reach the end of each stage.
  • Defeat the final boss to complete the adventure.

🕹️ Controls

Desktop

Use A/D or the Left/Right Arrow keys to move. Press Space or W to jump. Press J to attack and K to use your shield.

Mobile

Use the on-screen buttons for movement, jumping, attacking and shielding.

🏰 Adventure Stages

Mini Knight contains several themed areas. Begin in the peaceful Green Kingdom, travel through the Dark Forest, enter the Skeleton Castle and eventually reach the dangerous Dragon Fortress.

  • 🌳 Green Kingdom
  • 🌲 Dark Forest
  • 💀 Skeleton Castle
  • 🌋 Lava Fortress
  • 🐉 Dragon Castle

👹 Monsters & Bosses

The kingdom is protected by different enemies. Small monsters are only the beginning. Later stages introduce stronger enemies and large boss creatures with more health and stronger attacks.

💰 Treasure & Score

Coins increase your score and reward exploration. Defeating enemies also gives points. Try to finish each stage while keeping as much health as possible and collect as much treasure as you can.

❓ FAQ

Is Mini Knight free?

Yes. Mini Knight is designed as a free browser game for GameWithFun.

Yes. The game includes dedicated touch controls for smartphones and tablets.

No. The game runs directly in a modern web browser.

Yes. Keyboard controls are included for desktop computers.
