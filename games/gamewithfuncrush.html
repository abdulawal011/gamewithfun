Game With Fun Crush - Free Match 3 Puzzle Game

Welcome to Game With Fun Crush, a colorful and entertaining free online match 3 puzzle game. Match three or more candies of the same color, create exciting combinations, earn points and complete level goals.

Game With Fun Crush is designed for players who enjoy simple puzzle gameplay and short casual gaming sessions. The game can be played directly in a modern web browser on compatible mobile phones, tablets and desktop devices.

How to Play Game With Fun Crush

To play Game With Fun Crush, swipe a candy horizontally or vertically toward a neighboring candy. When three or more matching candies are connected, they disappear from the board and new candies fall into the empty spaces.

Try to plan your moves carefully. Consecutive matches can create combos and increase your score. Your goal is to complete the level task before your available moves run out.
