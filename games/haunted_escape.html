How to Play Haunted Escape

Your goal is to explore the haunted maze, collect every required key, and reach the locked exit. A monster roams the corridors and can chase you when it spots you. Watch your surroundings, listen for warning sounds, and use hiding places when danger gets close.

  • Explore the corridors and look for the glowing keys.
  • Collect all required keys before trying to open the exit door.
  • Use the flashlight to see farther, but remember that visibility affects how the monster detects you.
  • Find a nearby closet and hide when you need to avoid danger.
  • On later levels, look for the silver key needed to pass the iron gate.
  • Reach the exit to clear the level and continue your escape.

Controls

PC / Keyboard

WASD or Arrow Keys: Move
F: Toggle flashlight
E or Space: Hide or leave a hiding place

Mobile / Touch

Drag on the left side of the game screen to move. Use the HIDE and LIGHT buttons on the right side. Landscape mode gives the maze more room.

Haunted Escape – A Dark Maze Survival Challenge

Haunted Escape is a free browser horror game from GameWithFun that combines maze exploration, careful movement, key collecting, flashlight management, and monster avoidance. You enter a mysterious house where the corridors form a maze and the exit will not open until you have found the keys. Every journey asks you to balance curiosity with caution. The quickest-looking path may lead you close to danger, while a quiet detour can give you time to think and plan.

Explore a Maze That Changes

Each new level generates a fresh maze layout, so learning one route will not automatically solve the next. You need to read the corridors as you move, remember where you have already searched, and keep track of possible routes back to the exit. The maze becomes more demanding as you advance, adding more space to explore and additional challenges to consider. This changing layout helps keep repeat attempts interesting because your route and decisions matter each time you enter.

Find the Keys Before the Exit

Keys are the central objective of Haunted Escape. Search the open corridors and collect the glowing items scattered throughout the maze. The exit door stays locked while you still need keys, so rushing directly to the door is not enough. Check side passages, keep an eye on the progress counter, and think about how to return safely after collecting an item. On later levels, a special silver key may also be required to open an iron gate. Finding it changes which routes are available and can make your escape plan more complicated.

Stay Aware of the Monster

The monster is the main threat inside the haunted house. It patrols the maze, and when it detects you, it can change direction and begin a chase. Walls and barriers can interrupt its view, so corners and corridors are important when deciding where to move. Pay attention to the atmosphere and sound cues, especially when the danger feels close. Standing still in an exposed corridor is risky; keep thinking about the nearest turn, alternate route, or hiding place instead of moving without a plan.

Use Your Flashlight Wisely

The flashlight helps you see more of the dark environment, but using it is a tactical choice rather than a reason to stop paying attention. Toggle it when you need to inspect a corridor, locate an item, or understand the route ahead. The game’s detection rules also make light management part of the challenge. Try to combine short periods of careful exploration with awareness of nearby exits and hiding places. You will often make better decisions when you know where you can go next rather than simply moving toward the brightest area.

Hiding Can Give You Time

Closets and other marked hiding spots offer a way to avoid being seen when you are close enough to use one. If you are not near a hiding place, the game will tell you that you cannot hide there, so notice useful spots while exploring instead of searching for one only at the last moment. Hiding is not a substitute for navigation: you still need to collect keys and reach the exit. Use it as one part of a larger plan, and leave your hiding place when it is safe to continue.

Tips for New Players

First, learn the controls in a quiet section of the maze. On a computer, use WASD or the arrow keys to move, F to toggle the flashlight, and E or Space to hide or leave a hiding place. On a phone or tablet, drag on the left side of the game screen to move and tap the HIDE or LIGHT buttons when needed. Landscape mode is recommended because the game’s play area is designed around a wide view. Move deliberately at intersections, keep track of the key counter, and avoid taking a route you cannot explain to yourself.

If the monster begins chasing you, use the maze layout to break its line of sight and head toward a route you already recognize. Do not forget the exit location while collecting the last key. In later levels, remember that the silver key and iron gate may affect your route. If a run ends early, treat it as practice: the next attempt will have a different maze, and the experience you gain from reading the corridors can help you make better decisions.
