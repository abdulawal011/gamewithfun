🔥 Lava Escape

Lava Escape is a fast-paced volcanic platform adventure created for GameWithFun. Guide your explorer through an ancient temple while a dangerous river of lava rises below. Jump between platforms, collect glowing crystals and avoid traps and mysterious volcanic monsters.

Each level is generated with a guaranteed playable route. As the adventure continues, platforms become more challenging, enemies become more common and the lava becomes faster.

🎮 How to Play

  • Move left and right across the volcanic platforms.
  • Jump over gaps and dangerous obstacles.
  • Use your double jump when necessary.
  • Collect blue crystals for bonus points.
  • Jump on monsters to defeat them.
  • Avoid spikes and the rising lava.
  • Reach the glowing portal at the end of each level.
  • Complete all levels to escape the volcanic temple.

🕹️ Controls

Desktop

Use the Left and Right Arrow keys or A and D to move. Press Space, W or the Up Arrow to jump. You can jump twice while airborne.

Mobile

Hold the Left or Right button to move. Tap the Jump button to jump.

💎 Crystal Collection

Glowing crystals are placed along the safest routes and optional areas. Each collected crystal gives bonus points. Try to collect them while keeping enough distance from the rising lava.

🌋 Rising Lava

The lava slowly rises during every level. It creates pressure and prevents the player from waiting indefinitely on one platform. Higher levels increase the lava speed, so quick movement becomes increasingly important.

👹 Volcanic Monsters

Later levels contain roaming volcanic monsters. Jumping on top of a monster defeats it and awards bonus points. Touching one from the side costs a life.

🏆 High Score

Lava Escape automatically saves your best score in the browser's local storage. Collect crystals, defeat monsters and finish levels quickly to improve your score.
