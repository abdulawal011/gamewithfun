🔥 Lava Escape

Lava Escape is a fast-paced browser adventure game from GameWithFun. Run through an ancient volcanic temple while dangerous lava rises behind you. Jump across platforms, avoid traps, collect crystals and survive for as long as possible.

Every run becomes more challenging as the temple gets faster and the lava rises higher. Your goal is simple: keep moving, stay alive and achieve the highest score possible.

🎮 How to Play

  • Move left and right to control your explorer.
  • Jump over gaps, rocks and dangerous traps.
  • Use double jump when you need extra height.
  • Collect crystals to increase your score.
  • Avoid lava and dangerous enemies.
  • Keep moving because the lava continuously rises.
  • Reach the end of each level to continue your adventure.

🕹️ Controls

Desktop

Use the Left and Right arrow keys or A and D to move. Press Space, W or the Up Arrow to jump.

Mobile

Use the on-screen Left, Jump and Right buttons. The game is designed to work on phones and tablets.

💎 Collect Crystals

Glowing crystals are scattered throughout the volcanic temple. Collecting them increases your score. Try to collect as many as possible without putting your character in danger.

🌋 Rising Lava

The lava is the biggest danger in Lava Escape. It slowly rises from below and forces you to keep moving. Standing still for too long can quickly turn a safe situation into a dangerous one.

🏆 High Score

Your best score is saved directly in your browser using local storage. Try to beat your previous record and become the ultimate Lava Escape champion.

Lava Escape combines classic platforming with an exciting survival challenge. The volcanic environment is filled with glowing lava, ancient stone platforms, crystals and dangerous obstacles. The simple controls make the game easy to start, while increasing speed and difficulty provide a challenge for players who want to improve their score.
