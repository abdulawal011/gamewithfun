Game With Fun AI VS Human 8 Ball Pool

Game With Fun AI VS Human 8 Ball Pool is a free browser-based billiards game designed for quick and enjoyable matches on mobile phones, tablets and desktop devices. Aim your cue, control your shot power and try to pocket the balls while competing against the computer opponent.

The game uses a responsive HTML5 game area so you can play directly in your web browser without installing an application.

How to Play 8 Ball Pool

Start the game and drag away from the cue ball to choose your shooting direction and power. Release your finger to take the shot.

The first legally pocketed group determines whether you play solids or stripes. Clear your group before attempting the black 8-ball.
