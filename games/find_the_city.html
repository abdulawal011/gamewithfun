LOST ROAD:
FIND THE CITY

Drive deep into Pine Hollow forest, follow an old map, collect fuel, and discover the distant city. Can you find your way before your tank runs dry?

Best played in landscape mode on mobile. Tap Start to open the original game menu, then start the engine.

How to Play

  • Explore the forest roads and use the map to understand the area.
  • Follow roads and landmarks to navigate toward the city.
  • Collect yellow fuel cans to refill your tank.
  • Avoid trees, potholes, and rough ground that can slow you down.
  • Reach the city to complete your journey.

Controls

Mobile

Use the on-screen arrows to steer, ▲ to accelerate, ■ to brake or reverse, and ✋ for the handbrake. Use the view and map buttons to change your perspective and check the route.

Keyboard

W / S or ↑ / ↓: drive · A / D or ← / →: steer · Space: handbrake · C: change view · M: open the map.

Explore the Unknown in Lost Road: Find the City

Lost Road: Find the City is a 3D driving and exploration game about finding your way through a remote forest. You begin in Pine Hollow with an old map, a rugged vehicle, and a limited supply of fuel. The destination is a city hidden beyond the trees, and reaching it requires careful driving, observation, and good decisions.

The forest is more than scenery. Winding roads, uneven terrain, trees, rocks, and potholes make every trip feel different. Driving quickly may save time, but rough ground can reduce your speed and make the journey harder. Keep an eye on the fuel gauge and look out for yellow fuel cans along the route.

Navigation is the heart of the adventure. Use the map to build a picture of the surrounding area, then look for landmarks that can help you stay oriented. The old tower, forest cabin, lake, roads, and distant city all help create a world that rewards exploration. Try to remember where you have been and make each turn with a purpose.

Fuel management is just as important as steering. Accelerating constantly consumes fuel, and travelling away from the road can make the journey less efficient. If you spot a fuel can, consider whether the detour is worthwhile. A little planning can be the difference between arriving in the city and stopping in the forest.

On desktop, keyboard controls let you accelerate, reverse, steer, and change the camera view. On mobile, the on-screen controls make it possible to drive using touch. Landscape orientation gives the road and surrounding environment more room on the screen. You can switch between camera views to see more of the trail or look around from inside the vehicle.

Every journey asks you to balance exploration with caution. The road may be easier to follow, while a shortcut across the terrain can be slower and riskier. Watch the surroundings, avoid obstacles, collect supplies when useful, and keep moving toward the destination. The goal is simple to understand, but reaching it takes attention and a steady driving style.

If you enjoy browser driving games, open-world exploration, navigation challenges, and discovering hidden places, Lost Road: Find the City offers a compact adventure directly in your browser. No separate game installation is needed; open the page, start the engine, and begin searching for the city.

Take your time, learn the route, and stay aware of your remaining fuel. The city is waiting somewhere beyond Pine Hollow. Start your engine and see whether you can find it.
