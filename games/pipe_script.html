🧩 Pipe Script – Connect the Pipes and Solve the Puzzle

Pipe Script is a fun and interactive puzzle game created for players who enjoy simple controls and logical challenges. The main objective is to rotate the pipe pieces and create a connected route between the starting point and the target.

🎮 How to Play Pipe Script

Starting the game is easy. Press the START THE GAME button and begin your first level. You will see a grid containing different pipe pieces. Tap or click a pipe to rotate it.

Your goal is to arrange the pipes so that they form a continuous connection from the ball to the green target.

💡 Tip: Look carefully at the connections around each pipe before rotating it. Planning the route can make difficult levels easier to solve.

🧠 Think Before You Rotate

Every pipe rotation can change the route. Look carefully at the surrounding pieces before making your next move. Try to understand how each piece connects with the others and build a complete path.

Some puzzles can be solved quickly, while others may require several moves and careful planning.

🌟 20 Levels to Complete

Pipe Script features 20 puzzle levels. Each level provides a different arrangement of pipe pieces, giving you a new path-building challenge.

After completing a level, press the NEXT LEVEL button to continue your journey.

📱 Play on Mobile and Desktop

The game is designed for both mobile and desktop browsers. On mobile devices, tap a pipe to rotate it. On desktop computers, click a pipe with your mouse.

No additional software or installation is required. The game runs directly in a supported web browser.
