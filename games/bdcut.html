BD Cut 🎮🍉 – Free Fruit Cutting Game Online

Welcome to BD Cut, a fun and simple fruit cutting browser game designed for quick and enjoyable gameplay. Cut falling fruits, create combos, avoid dangerous bombs and try to make your highest score.

BD Cut works directly in your web browser, so you can play without installing a separate application. The game is designed to work comfortably on mobile phones, tablets and desktop browsers.

About BD Cut Fruit Game

BD Cut is an original HTML5 fruit slicing game where different fruits fall from the top of the game screen. Your goal is to slice the fruits before they reach the bottom.

Each successfully sliced fruit gives you points. Cutting several fruits can increase your combo. However, bombs are mixed with the fruits, so you need to react carefully and avoid slicing them.

The game is made for casual entertainment and short gaming sessions. You can replay the game as many times as you want and try to improve your previous high score.

How to Play BD Cut

Playing BD Cut is easy. Follow these simple steps:

  • Press the START GAME button.
  • Wait for fruits to appear from the top.
  • Swipe your finger across the fruit to cut it.
  • Each fruit gives you 10 points.
  • Cut multiple fruits to build your combo.
  • Avoid bombs because slicing a bomb ends the game.
  • Try not to let too many fruits fall to the bottom.
  • Replay the game and try to beat your best score.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is BD Cut?

BD Cut is a free online fruit cutting game. Players slice falling fruits and try to achieve a high score while avoiding bombs.

Is BD Cut free to play?

Yes. BD Cut is designed as a free browser game and does not require a paid game download.

Can I play BD Cut on a mobile phone?

Yes. The game uses touch-friendly controls and is designed for modern mobile browsers.

How do I get a higher score?

Slice fruits quickly and accurately, maintain combos and avoid bombs. Your best score is saved locally in your browser.

Cutting a bomb immediately ends the current game.
