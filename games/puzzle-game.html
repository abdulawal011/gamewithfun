Game With Fun Picture Puzzle

Game With Fun Picture Puzzle is a simple and entertaining online sliding puzzle game that you can play directly in your web browser. The goal is to arrange the numbers from 1 to 8 in the correct order by moving tiles into the empty space.

This free puzzle game is designed for both mobile and desktop players. You do not need to download an application or install special software. Simply open the game, tap or click the tiles and start solving the puzzle.

How to Play the Picture Puzzle

The puzzle contains eight numbered tiles and one empty space. Only a tile next to the empty space can be moved. Your objective is to place every number in the correct sequence.

  • Tap or click a tile beside the empty space.
  • Move the tiles until they form the correct order.
  • Try to complete the puzzle with fewer moves.
  • Use the Shuffle button to start a new puzzle.
  • Check your Best score and try to improve it.
