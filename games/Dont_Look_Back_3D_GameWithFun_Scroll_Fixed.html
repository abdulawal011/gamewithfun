🎮 How to Play DON'T LOOK BACK 3D

DON'T LOOK BACK 3D is a first-person horror survival and escape game. Your goal is to explore an abandoned house, find important items, unlock restricted areas, discover the three digits of the final exit code and escape the building.

Explore the House

When the game begins, carefully explore the rooms and hallways. The house contains different areas and several locked doors. Important items are placed around the environment, so searching carefully is essential. Do not assume that the first room contains everything you need.

Find the Keys

There are different locked doors inside the house. Search for the required keys and use them to unlock new areas. Opening a door can give you access to another part of the house and bring you closer to the information needed for your escape.

Find the Three Code Notes

Three notes contain the three digits needed for the final exit lock. Look carefully around the house and remember the order of the digits. You need to discover all three pieces of information before you can correctly open the final exit.

Watch for the Creature

You are not alone inside the house. A creature moves through the environment and can begin following you when it detects you. Footsteps and other audio events can warn you that danger may be nearby.

Use Hiding Places

Wardrobes, lockers and closets can be used as hiding places. If the creature is searching for you, finding a suitable hiding location can give you time to stay out of its path.

Use the Map

The map can help you understand the structure of the house. It shows important areas, locked doors, hiding locations and the exit. Use it when you become unsure about your location or need to plan your next route.

Reach the Exit

After collecting the necessary keys and discovering all three code digits, make your way to the exit. Enter the correct combination and escape the house.

PC Controls

  • W / A / S / D: Move
  • Arrow Keys: Move
  • Mouse: Look around
  • E / F: Hide or leave hiding place
  • M: Open map

Mobile Controls

  • Left side: Virtual joystick for movement
  • Right side: Drag to look around
  • HIDE: Hide or leave a hiding place
  • Map: Open the house map
