🎮 How to Play Shadow Hill Climb

Shadow Hill Climb is a manual hill-climbing driving game. Unlike an automatic runner, your vehicle does not move forward by itself. You must control the vehicle and decide when to accelerate, brake and reverse.


  • Tap GAS ▶ to accelerate forward.
  • Tap ◀ BRAKE / REVERSE to slow down or move backward.
  • When the vehicle becomes airborne, the controls help balance it.
  • Collect coins to increase your score.
  • Collect fuel to continue your journey.
  • Travel as far as possible and beat your best distance.

🕹️ Controls

Mobile: Use the two large buttons shown on the screen.

Desktop:

  • ➡ Arrow Right / D = Gas
  • ⬅ Arrow Left / A = Brake / Reverse
  • Space = Pause

🌑 About Shadow Hill Climb

Shadow Hill Climb is a browser-based driving game created for GameWithFun. The game uses a dark shadow-inspired visual style combined with procedural hills, coins, fuel pickups and simple physics.


The main goal is to travel across the hills while keeping control of your vehicle. The terrain changes throughout the journey, so players need to manage their speed carefully.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is the car automatic?

No. Shadow Hill Climb uses manual driving. The vehicle will not continuously accelerate by itself.


Can I play on mobile?

Yes. The game includes large touch controls designed for mobile devices.


Does the game use fullscreen?

When you press START, the game requests fullscreen and landscape orientation where the browser supports those features.


What happens if landscape lock is not supported?

The game displays a rotate-your-phone message so you can manually turn the device sideways.

Shadow Hill Climb – Free Online Hill Racing Game

Shadow Hill Climb is a free online browser racing game from GameWithFun where players drive a shadow-style vehicle across challenging hills. The game focuses on manual control rather than automatic movement, giving players direct control over acceleration, braking and reversing.


The landscape is designed around rolling hills and changing terrain. Players must carefully manage their speed while driving across slopes. Coins are placed throughout the world and can be collected during the journey. Fuel pickups also appear along the route and help the player continue playing.


Shadow Hill Climb is designed for both mobile and desktop browsers. On mobile devices, the game provides large touch controls. On desktop computers, players can use the keyboard. The game also attempts to switch into fullscreen landscape mode when the player starts the game, creating a larger gameplay area.


The objective is simple: drive as far as possible, collect coins, manage fuel and keep the vehicle under control. Since the vehicle does not automatically drive forward, every movement is controlled by the player. This makes speed management an important part of the gameplay.
