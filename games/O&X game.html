O&X Game - Free Online Tic Tac Toe Game

Welcome to O&X Game, a simple and fun online strategy game inspired by the classic O and X board game. Play directly in your browser without downloading or installing anything. You can challenge the AI or play with a friend on the same device.

O&X Game is designed for quick and enjoyable gameplay on mobile phones and desktop computers. Choose X or O, select your preferred difficulty level and try to complete three matching symbols in a row before your opponent.

How to Play O&X Game

  • Choose Play With AI to challenge the computer.
  • Choose Play With Friend for two-player gameplay.
  • Select X or O when playing against the AI.
  • Tap an empty square to place your symbol.
  • Try to create three matching symbols in a horizontal, vertical or diagonal line.
  • Use the New Round button to start another game.
  • Use Undo to take back your previous move.
