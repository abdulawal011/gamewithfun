🎮 How to Play Spider Night Vision

Spider Night Vision is a fast and simple endless city-swinging game designed for both mobile and desktop browsers. Your objective is to travel as far as possible while collecting glowing energy orbs and protecting your health. The city becomes increasingly challenging as you continue playing, so quick reactions and good timing are important.

When the game begins, your character appears in a dark futuristic city surrounded by tall buildings, glowing windows and a night sky. The special night-vision presentation gives the entire game a green tactical appearance. You will see energy orbs floating through the city and drones moving through the air. Your score increases while you continue moving, and collecting an energy orb gives you an additional score bonus.

The main movement mechanic is the web button. Hold the web button on a mobile device or hold the Space key on a desktop keyboard to activate the web swing. Releasing the button lets your character fall and continue moving through the environment. Use the left and right controls to adjust your horizontal position and line up with energy orbs while avoiding obstacles.

Your health bar is displayed at the top of the game. Colliding with enemy drones reduces your health. Staying too low for too long can also become dangerous, so keep your character moving and use the web mechanic to control your position. Once your health reaches zero, the mission ends and your final score, distance and collected coins are shown.

There is no complicated installation process. The game is designed to run directly in a modern web browser. You can play on a phone, tablet or computer. The controls are intentionally simple so that you can start playing quickly without needing to learn a large number of buttons.

🎯 Tips and Tricks

1. Keep Moving

The city continuously moves toward you, so staying active is important. Watch the position of your character and make small adjustments rather than waiting until an obstacle is extremely close.

2. Use the Web Carefully

Holding the web button changes your movement. Learn how long you need to hold it to reach a comfortable height. Releasing it at the right moment can help you control your descent.

3. Watch the Drones

Enemy drones can reduce your health. If a drone is approaching your position, use horizontal movement and the web mechanic together to create distance.

4. Collect Energy Orbs

Energy orbs are valuable because they increase your coin count and provide additional points. However, do not put your entire run at risk for one orb. Survival and distance are also important.

5. Improve Your High Score

The game becomes faster over time. Your first goal can be learning the controls, followed by surviving longer and collecting more energy. Once you become comfortable with the movement, challenge yourself to set a new personal best.
